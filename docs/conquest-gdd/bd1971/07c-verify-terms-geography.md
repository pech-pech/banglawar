## 2. Monsoon and flood season

| Claim in our notes | Finding | Verdict | Src |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monsoon June to September | Wikipedia (Climate of Bangladesh, citing the Bangladesh Meteorological Department six-season scheme) and the Geography of Bangladesh page give monsoon June to September, about 80% of annual rain. A Bangladesh Meteorological Department-linked abstract in the academic literature (search summary) gives four seasons: pre-monsoon March to May, monsoon June to September, post-monsoon October to November, winter December to February; it says more than 71% of annual rain falls in the monsoon | VERIFIED for June to September. Share of rain: 71% or more (meteorological paper) versus about 80% (Wikipedia, Library of Congress): say "roughly 70 to 80%" | 14, 15, 16, 17 |
| Alternative April to October | The phrase "April to October" appears in Wikipedia Operation Jackpot: "during the April-October monsoon flooding, about 300 additional navigable channels". This is the origin of our two items | CONTRADICTED as the meteorological monsoon. April and May are pre-monsoon; the wet-season usage (April to October) is a looser agricultural or river-flow sense. Use June to September for the climate monsoon; say "rainy and flood season, roughly April to October" only if quoting the Jackpot page | 18, 14 |
| Onset and withdrawal dates | A study of 1990 to 2019 data from 11 stations (search summary of a refereed abstract): onset near extreme south-east about 10 June; covers the country in about 14 days; withdrawal begins in the north-west about 4 October, takes about 16 days, average date about 20 October; season length 102 to 132 days; standard deviation 7 to 8 days (onset) and 5 to 7 days (withdrawal) | PARTLY. Good evidence for "arrives about 10 June in the south-east, gone by about 20 October on average". | Flood season for haors July to November | Wikipedia Haor: "During July to November ... go under deep water", under water about seven months | VERIFIED (one source plus indirect). Banglapedia haor page not readable | 10 |

The November-to-April span is the dry season in common usage (boro rice grown in haors in the dry season) | PARTLY. "December to February dry winter, March to May hot pre-monsoon with thunderstorms" is supported; "dry season November to April" is the common usage but not stated in a fetched source | 14, 10 |

## 6. Corrections to carry into files 01 and 02

1. Highest point: replace "Saka Haphong / Mowdok Mual ~986 m" with "Saka Haphong, about 1,052 m (SRTM; 1,063 m also published); Keokradong about 986 m is not the highest".
2. "Mukti Fauj ... trained by India": change to "early name for the regular force (Army, EPR), later Niyomito Bahini".
3. Radio: start date 26 to 27 March (declarations) versus 3 April (Wikipedia first broadcast). Say "declarations of 26 and 27 March from Chittagong; the station began regular broadcasts in April and relocated to Tripura".
4. "300 extra navigable channels": downgrade to unverified; separate from "300+ guerrilla operations on rivers" (RDRC study, Dhaka Tribune).
5. Monsoon: use June to September for climate; April to October is the looser rainy and river-flow season. Onset about 10 June in the south-east; withdrawal about 20 October on average (30-year study).
6. Hilli: Rangpur Division today (Rajshahi Division in 1971); Bhairab is Kishoreganj (Dhaka Division), Ashuganj is Brahmanbaria.
7. Divisions: label that Rangpur, Sylhet, Mymensingh and Barisal divisions did not exist in 1971.
