## What the scores say
1. **Viewpoint:** a steep, high, nearly overhead view of a bounded slab or tile field scored best (W4 and W5 both 5 on Seedream; W5 5 on Kling). The low 30-degree camera (W3) and the picture with a horizon and sky (Seedream W2) scored 2: the wider "scene" framing lost points.
2. **Colour and lighting:** the only 5/5 pair is Seedream W2: overcast, even soft light with slate blues and blue-greens. Warm sun-lit versions (dawn W1, golden W3) scored only 3. Kling's olive ground and vignette scored the lowest on both (2.6 and 2.4).
3. **Model:** Seedream 4.5 wins on overall, colour and lighting; Kling 3 Omni wins on viewpoint (cleaner framing of the world slab) at two-thirds of the price.
4. **Best overall single image:** Seedream W4 (sea-to-hills, steep overhead), with lighting the one weak score (3).

## Full-tile round (sent 2026-10-04, Seedream 4.5, 30 credits, balance 4,209 to 4,179; audit passes)

Brief from the owner: let forests show the density and outlook of the bamboo-and-tree grove in N4; tiles must look full. Prompt reworked with the prompt-engineer slots: every tile "completely filled edge to edge with its terrain, with no bare tile"; forest tiles with "tall faceted bamboo stems and rounded trees ... crowns overlap into one thick green mass"; hill tiles that fill most of the tile and stand taller than the trees; the tiles float alone (no sea or land beyond the edge); no rain.

| Image | Direction | Overall | Viewpoint | Colour | Lighting | Total |

Notes by the assistant (not the owner's scores): the "full tiles" wording worked (dense bamboo, tight tree groves, planted paddy, thick reeds and lily pads, crowded mangrove roots, rounded hills taller than the trees). Fault: "only dark gaps" produced black voids in the forest canopies (a dark blob with holes in M1, black holes in the large crowns in M2); next time say "crowns overlap with no gaps". Also in both: a few faint rain streaks, and the paddy tiles in M1 read as sunken trays with a thin rim.

Ranking by total across all 17 images: N5 20, M2 19, M1 18, N1 18, N4 18. Concept spend to date: 650 credits (420 + 125 + 45 + 30 + 30); balance 4,179.
