## 1. Style target (R8a, viewed)
R8a shows: smooth, balloon-like cloud crowns on thin forked trunks (not faceted), tall fan-leaf palms, a dense bamboo clump, dark-thatch and straw-thatch huts with board walls, open bamboo-post shelters with thatch, round granaries on stilts with a conical roof, a plank jetty on posts, tea hills with ridged rows, paddy tiles. Colours are bright and saturated, surfaces soft with fine thatch grain. Consequence: Quaternius's rounded "stylised" kits fit the crowns and palms best; Kenney is cleaner and more toy-like; hard-edged Poly Pizza/Sketchfab models fit worst and need smoothing and recolouring. (S2 was not opened; fit remarks are against R8a.)

## 6. Procedure reminders (from 02-open-assets.md, still binding)
Licence record per asset (title, author, URL, licence, date, SHA-256, approver) in art-src/bd1971-3d/sources/; manual download only; prefer glTF/FBX/OBJ over .blend; open untrusted .blend with auto-run scripts off; delete figures, flags, text and logos on import and record the removal; CC BY items need a line "[name] by [author], CC BY x.x, [URL], changes made"; re-read each licence page on the day of download because summaries here came through a page summariser.
