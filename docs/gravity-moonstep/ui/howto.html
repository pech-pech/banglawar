How to play
step from moon to moon, ahead of the tide
1Hold

Press and hold. A gold ring charges as the pearl gathers power.

2Aim

Drag to bend the dotted arc. The silver ring marks where you would land.

3Release

Let go to launch. Catch the next moon’s pull and keep climbing.

Flick
Quick flick reverses your orbit direction.
Drag down
Drag back below your thumb to cancel the shot.
