## Concrete per-template fixes
- t02 (Charge Banking, needs 5 inputs, windows 1,1,1,3,18): needs all 5 inputs to be near-exact. Widen the crumble or gate target: make the gate slot at least 12 px taller and the landing ledge at least 22 px longer so each flip tolerates +-6 ticks; or cut the intended solution to 4 inputs by removing one bank step.
- t05 (windows 1,7,3): first flip window of 1 tick after tick 0 means the second input must land exactly. Lengthen the ledge it lands on by about 30 px or lower the speed at that point by moving the first gap closer.
- t06, t07, t08 (windows 2-3): the second input in each is a corner or wall flip with a 2-3 tick window; add 20 px of wall length past the corner (or move the spike off the corner by 12 px) to give >=6 ticks.
- t14 (Gate Punch with crumble, 5 inputs, windows 1,1,3): the gate threshold of 120 px/s with a crumble timer of 0.35 s leaves almost no slack; widen the crumble ledge (currently 100 wide at x200) or start the dive earlier by placing the ledge 40 px higher so the bob reaches GATE_V with margin.
- t17, t18 (windows 1): the first input needs the bob at a precise tick after tick 0; add a 20 px lip on the starting ledge so the flip is not racing the edge.
- t19 (crusher bars, windows 1-5): both bars slide +-60 px on 2.4 s periods with phases only 1.6 rad apart, so a gap is open only about 3-5 ticks. Increase the vertical spacing of the bars from 100 px to 140 px, or reduce one bar's amp to 40.
- t16 (two presses, unmirrored windows 3,3): the pair of presses 0.5 rad apart leaves a short common-open window; widen the phase difference to about 1.0 rad or shorten the presses from 20 px to 14 px.
- t03, t13 (crusher over a 16 px side gap): works with a 14-18 tick window but only after the press goes up; the 16 px lane between ledge end (x112) and press (x128) is below the 22 px bob, so the only route is waiting for the press to rise. State that in the teach text, or open the lane to 30 px.
- t04, t09, t10 (windows 4-6): acceptable for tier 1 only if the failing case is a clean bounce; widen by about 10 px on the landing target.
- t01, t11, t12, t13, t15, t20: fine as they are.
